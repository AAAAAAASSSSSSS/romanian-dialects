# Ardelenesc (Transylvanian varieties)
Transylvanian Romanian is a mosaic of small transition varieties (Weigand 1908; Petrovici: older settlement = more fragmentation). Crișana and Maramureș are the most distinct; two weaker areas: north-east and centre/south. G. Ivănescu counts north-east, centre and south as separate graiuri (NE-TRA). The app's "Ardelenesc" entry = features shared by the group (EN-TRA) + the north-east labial pattern (NE-TRA).
## Implemented (✔)
- ✔ after s, z, ț: e→ă, i→î, ea→a (EN-TRA: in some varieties also after ș, j, r — not implemented)
- ✔ ɨj→ɨ: câine, mâine, pâine → cîne, mîne, pîne
- ✔ oa→o (bolă, codă, gropă, aprope, domnă) with the semivowel dropped (NE-TRA, citing Rusu 1984)
- ✔ a durmi / a adurmi with u in the whole paradigm (dorm→durm)
- ✔ NE Transylvania: p→pt, b→bd, m→mń; f→ș, v→z before i (fin→șin, fierbinte→șierbinte, vin→zin, vițel→zițăl)
## Not implemented
- stressed e,ə,o open to ɛ,ɜ,ɔ; stressed vowels longer ("impression of singing", NE-TRA); a before stressed syllable closes to ă (pahar→păhar); initial-syllable stress in centre and west (súspin, grădină, Émil, Ștéfan); stress shift to the root in some verbs (blástăm, înfășur, strécur, mășur).
- Labial palatalization has several stages in the north-east: pt', bd' / pĉ, pk', bĝ, bg', mń (pĉatră, albĝină, nimńic); the app uses only pt/bd/mń.
## Morphology (recorded)
- no -ez/-esc in some conj. I/IV verbs: lucră/lucru, prăd, săr, se ospătă, se învîrte; iotacized verbs with -n/-r stems: să spuie, spui, țîu, să țîie.
## Lexis: Hungarian loans darab(ă), gazdă, paprică; German loans fain(ă), maistăr, șuștăr, tișlăr (NE-TRA examples).
## Caveat: the Bârgău article is a methodology paper that summarizes older literature; the NE-TRA features are secondhand.
