# Timok Romanian (graiul timocean / Vlach) — OEAW, RO-BAN
~170 settlements in Eastern Serbia (plus NW Bulgaria). Two main groups: **Ungureni** (west of the Morava–Crna Reka–Danube–Timok area, Banat-like, arrived 18th–19th c. from Banat) and **Țărani** (east: Ključ and Negotin Krajina, similar to western Oltenia). A third and fourth group exist (Hutanu & Sorescu-Marinković 2015) and a mixed speech where the two live together.
## Implemented
- Ungureni: ce/ci→șʲ (cinci→șʲinșʲi), te/ti→ce/ci (frunte→frunce), internal de→ge (unde→unge), di→ghi (dinte→ghince). From the RO-BAN snippet and OEAW's statement that the t,d and c,g difference is the main one between the groups.
- Țărani: Oltenian vowel shift after sibilants and j insertion (ol_* rules), as OEAW says they resemble western Oltenia. Weak support.
## Not implemented / open
- "dies>dzî" (dz) in Ungureni, n palatalization, c+u hardening (RO-BAN snippet, unclear), neutralization of retroflex/alveolo-palatal affricates (Hutanu & Sorescu-Marinković 2015, the -ešće suffix), heavy Serbian loanwords, orthographies (Vlach National Council script, Cyrillic + Latin, 2012; first Vlach grammar in Serbian; Gospel translation).
- The Neiescu–Beltechi–Mocanu atlas (2006) records labial palatalization in certain contexts and preservation of palatal fricatives.
- Do not label Timok data as complete: I found no full phonetic description.
