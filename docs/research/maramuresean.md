# Maramureș (graiul maramureșean)
Source: EN-MAR (full article), FEL, Coteanu via Felecan. Few distinctive features; many are shared with Crișana and other Transylvanian varieties; treated as a separate dialect since Todoran and Coteanu, as a "grai of a transition zone" by Coteanu.
## Implemented (✔) from EN-MAR
- ✔ f→s and v→z before i (să fie→să sie, vierme→zierme; FEL: Weigand 1908 "sier = fier, zin = vin"; the Tratat notes younger speakers say sin where old ones said fin).
- ✔ p→pt, b→bd, m→mn before i (piele→ptiele, bine→bdine, mic→mnic); l, n palatalized before front vowels (lemne, vine).
- ✔ after s, z, ș, j, ț: front vowels become central and ea→a (singur→sîngur, seară→sară, zeamă→zamă, jir→jîr, țin→țîn, zi→dzî).
- ✔ after a word-initial labial: e→ă (merg→mărg, meargă→margă, pe→pă).
- ✔ oa→o (ușoară, noapte); stressed final ea→é (avea, vrea); ia→ie in băiat, muiat; de→di; câine/mâine/pâine→cîńe/mîńe/pîńe.
- ✔ archaic dz/dʒ kept in a few words: zic→dzîc, jos→gios, joc→gioc.
## Not implemented
- Mid vowels e, ə, o closing toward i, ɨ, u (general tendency); e→ɛ when e appears in two consecutive syllables (fete); devocalized final i/u (păcurar, cer); ɨ preserved in îmblu, îmflu, întru; c, g less palatal and centralizing following e (cer→[tʃər]).
## Morphology and lexis (recorded)
- invariable possessive a meu...; 1st and 4th conjugation verbs without -ez/-esc (lucră, mă rușin) but -esc in împărțăsc, omorăsc, simțăsc; n replaced in spuj, viw, viind; auxiliary o (singular) and or/o (plural): o dzîs, or dzîs; să deie/steie/beie/vreie; analytic pluperfect m-am fost dus; imperatives adă, vină; shortened forms (o fo, Gheo); words: a cușăi (a gusta), cocon (copil), pup (boboc), potică (farmacie), zierme (șarpe); demonstratives aista, aiasta.
- Sub-areas: wide central part; NW with Oaș influence; SE.
## Weigand/Felecan: ě appears as in Crișana (căměșe, měle, spěle, mě, bě, vrě) per Coteanu (as quoted by Felecan).
