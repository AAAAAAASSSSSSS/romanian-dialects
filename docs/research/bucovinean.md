# Bukovinian (graiul bucovinean)
EN-BUK has no phonetic section. All phonetics below are from TUR-77 (Turculeț 1977–78), partly confirmed by the en.wikipedia summary (mixture of Moldavian, Transylvanian and Maramureș features; faster tempo, stronger stress, aphonized or dropped unstressed final syllables).
## Four main areas (+ Eastern in EN-BUK)
1. **Dornean** (Țara Dornelor): transition between NE Transylvanian and Moldavian; Transylvanian features thin out toward the SE. Features: africatized palatals and dentals, closing of final -ă/-e grows southward; curastă; zi→? analogical o; ps→pt (capță); oa→o in Cârlibaba and Coșna.
2. **Câmpulungean** (Câmpulung, Fundu Moldovei, Moldovița, Sucevița, Suha): the most archaic. Keeps affricates ce/ci [tʃ, dʒ] (no fricativization); labials palatalized as f→h', v→ʝ; labial kept in pk', bg', mn' (pchi, bghi, mni); no e→ă after s, z, ș, j, ț (rece, aș, coș, șed); traces of rhotacism (pîră, pîrei); ropoi; rătă; şer/sin: şerin; serin; perină. Pojorâta/Fundu Moldovei show later Transylvanian-type dental palatalization; Pojorâta/Vatra Moldoviței show recent "moldovenizare" (closing of final -ă/-e).
3. **Rădăuțean** (Rădăuți–Siret–Solca–Suceava valley): Moldavian-type with archaic traits and variable Transylvanian layers: fricativization of c, g; palatalization of f, v; loss of labial in pk', bg', mn' (recent, advancing); closing of final -ă/-e consistent in the east; dipi (după), zespe (viespe), adverb aș, genitive-dative -li (capuli), shortened vocatives (tată→tat!), apocopated verb forms (grăj), tendency toward Maramureș-type features in the NW (Straja–Frătăuții Noi).
4. **Southeast "africatizantă"** (Gura Humorului–Botoșana–Tătăruși, Suceava area): Transylvanian layer from the 18th century over an older Câmpulungean-type base; primary c, g → s, z; secondary c, g (from chi/ghi) and dentals t,d before e,i → c, g (frunce, gince), with island speech varieties keeping the archaic type around Suceava (Bosanci, Costâna, Pătrăuți, Mihoveni).
## Implemented
- Câmpulung: pi→pchi, bi→bghi, mi→mni, fi→hi.
- Rădăuți: c/g→ș/j, labial loss (chi, ghi, ni), final -ă→-î, -e→-i, lexical dipi, zespe.
- Southeast/Dorna: c/g→ș/j, then chi/ghi→ci/gi, t→c, d→g before e/i.
## Not implemented: per-village mosaic; closing of final vowels in the SE; Dornean fine detail; speech tempo and syllable dropping.
